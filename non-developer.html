Publications

Eye-Tracking Phishing Attacks: Comparing Behavioral Responses to AI- and Human-Crafted Emails

IEEE International Conference on Computing, Communication and Networking Technologies (ICCCNT), 2025 · Jul 7, 2025

This study investigates how users respond to phishing emails generated by AI (GPT-3) versus human writers using eye-tracking metrics. Participants exhibited longer visual fixation and higher cognitive scrutiny toward human-crafted emails, despite both types achieving high deception rates. The analysis revealed statistically significant differences in gaze behavior across email regions, highlighting the nuanced threat posed by AI-generated phishing and the role of visual attention in cyber deception. These findings suggest new directions for phishing detection and user-awareness training systems.
